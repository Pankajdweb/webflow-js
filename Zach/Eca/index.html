Why now

Surgery is moving outpatient. ECA builds the instruments for where it's going.

70% of surgeries are now moving to the outpatient setting — more than 20% of them orthopedic.

The ambulatory surgery centers doing this work run lean: limited storage, lean staffing, and little sterile processing capacity. The reusable tray model was built for hospitals, who had a full sterile processing department down the hall.

That's why the instrument's price tag is the wrong number to watch. A reusable tray touches transport, inspection, cleaning, sterilization, storage, and staging before every case. Surgery-Ready™ kits remove that entire chain: open, use, dispose.

Which makes switching a systems decision, not just a product decision. The procedural kit is the delivery vehicle. The platform is the value.

Reusable Tray, Every Case

Surgery-Ready™, Every Case
Open→ Use→ Dispose
Six handling steps collapse into three — every case starts sterile, calibrated, and complete.
